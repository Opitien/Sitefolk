Frequently asked questions

Do you provide emergency plumbing?

Yes. We handle emergency call-outs for leaks, burst pipes, blocked drains and other urgent plumbing problems. Call us on 0161 555 0182 and we'll do our best to attend as quickly as possible.

How quickly can you attend?

For emergencies, we aim to respond the same day wherever possible. For general bookings, we'll arrange a time that suits you, usually within a few working days.

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes. We're happy to provide a free, no-obligation quote for any plumbing or heating work. Get in touch using the form below or give us a call.

Are you fully insured?

Yes. We carry full public liability insurance for your peace of mind. We're also happy to provide documentation on request.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Manchester and cover the wider Greater Manchester area including Salford, Didsbury, Chorlton, Sale, Altrincham, Stockport and Trafford.
