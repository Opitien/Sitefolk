1. The biggest missing piece: the actual conversion mechanism

Right now the page repeatedly says things like:

“Choose Essential →”

and

“Choose Pro →”

but the page needs to answer:

“Okay, I like this. What happens now?”

Especially because you're targeting businesses from Yell.

I'd make the primary CTA something like:

Get Your Website →

Then take them to a very short form:

Business name
Website / Yell listing
Name
Email
What does your business do?
Which plan interests you?
Submit

That's much better for cold outreach than forcing someone into a meeting.
